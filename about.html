Our story

Comfort, considered.

Founded in November 2023 by Mr Endurance, Favour Residencies was created for guests who want more from an apartment stay: privacy when they need it, warmth when they want it, and reliable essentials throughout.

Every detail, from the layout of each room to the shared spaces around it, is chosen to make short visits feel effortless and longer stays feel familiar.

Fast, reliable internet keeps workdays moving, so guests can enjoy the calm without giving up the connection.
